Who we are

Design is not our job. It's our passion.

Infurn Designs Studio is an interior design and bespoke furniture practice based in Gurugram. We believe a space should feel effortless — and that effortlessness is the result of hundreds of small, deliberate decisions.

✦Studio project

Our story

A studio built around craft.

We started Infurn with a simple conviction: interiors are only as good as their details. Over the years we've grown into a full-service studio — designing homes, offices and retail spaces, and building the furniture that completes them.

Because design and making live under one roof, nothing gets lost in translation. The piece you see in the 3D view is the piece we build and install. That control is how we protect quality — and your time.

What guides us

Principles we don't compromise.

01

Detail first

Joinery lines, reveals, hardware, lighting temperature — we sweat the details most people only feel.

02

Honest materials

Real timber, stone, metal and textile, chosen for how they age and how you'll actually use them.

03

One accountable team

Design, procurement and site execution in-house, so there's a single point of ownership from brief to handover.

04

Made to measure

Furniture designed for your space and proportions — never forced to fit around a catalogue.

05

Calm process

Clear stages, clear drawings, clear budgets. Fewer surprises, better decisions.

06

Built to last

We specify and construct for longevity — spaces meant to look right for years, not months.
