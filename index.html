The Studio

A design practice where detail is the whole point.

We are a multidisciplinary interior design and furniture studio based in Gurugram. From a single statement piece to a complete turnkey home, we handle concept, drawings, material selection and on-site execution — so the finished space matches the drawing, exactly.

  • End-to-end delivery Concept, 3D visualisation, procurement and site supervision under one roof.
  • Made-to-measure furniture Designed in-studio and built by trusted craftspeople.
  • Honest materials Considered timber, stone, metal and textile, specified for how you live.

How we work

A calm, clear process.

Discover

We listen — how you live or work, what you love, your budget and timeline. Site visit and measurements follow.

Design

Concept, layouts, material palettes and photoreal 3D views. We refine together until it feels right.

Craft

Bespoke furniture and joinery are built while site works progress under our supervision.

Deliver

Finishing, styling and a clean handover of a space that matches the drawing, down to the detail.
